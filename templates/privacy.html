Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

AthletixAI ("we", "us") is an AI sports talent assessment platform owned and operated by Archita Tripathi and Manthan Dhanraj. This policy explains, in plain language, what data we collect, why we collect it, and the control you have over it. We have written this to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

The short version: Your training videos are analysed on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers. We store only your account details and the numeric performance scores produced by the analysis. You can download or permanently delete everything at any time from the Privacy Center inside the app.

1. Data we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number (optional), password (stored only as a bcrypt hash, never in readable form), and your role (athlete or coach).
  • Athlete profile — sport, age, and whether your area is rural or urban. This is used for benchmarking and talent discovery.
  • Coach profile — specialty, experience, city, bio and achievements, which are shown publicly to athletes so they can choose a coach.
  • Performance data — the numeric scores generated from your session (Speed, Agility, Strength, Stamina, Technique), the date, and the sport. These are numbers only.
  • Platform activity — messages you send, coach ratings you give, notifications, and basic login records (time and IP address) kept for account security.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not upload or store your videos. Pose analysis runs entirely in your browser using on-device computer vision. Only the resulting numbers are sent to our server.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location, contacts, or any biometric identifiers such as face templates or fingerprints.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Why we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, and to let you log in.
  • To generate your performance reports and track your progress over time.
  • To rank athletes on the leaderboard and let verified coaches discover talent.
  • To send essential emails: email verification, password reset, and security alerts.
  • To improve our scoring models — but only if you have switched on the benchmarking or research consent in the Privacy Center, and only in aggregated, non-identifying form.

4. Children and young athletes

Many of our users are under 18. We take this seriously.

  • If you are under 18, you must have permission from a parent or legal guardian to use AthletixAI. Under the DPDP Act, verifiable parental consent is required for users below 18 years of age.
  • We do not show behavioural advertising to any user, and we never track children for advertising purposes.
  • Only verified coaches can initiate contact with an athlete. All messages are logged on the platform and can be reviewed.
  • A parent or guardian may request access to, correction of, or deletion of a minor's data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your rights and controls

Inside the app, open Privacy Center to:

  • Manage consent — turn benchmarking, research and marketing consent on or off at any time.
  • Download your data — export everything we hold about you as a JSON file.
  • Delete your account — this permanently removes your profile, reports, messages and ratings from our database. It cannot be undone.

You may also request correction of inaccurate information, or withdraw consent, by emailing us.

6. How we protect your data

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are never stored or transmitted in readable form.
  • All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies; all state-changing requests are protected against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).
  • Database queries are parameterised to prevent SQL injection, and repeated failed logins are rate-limited.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your personal data, we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board as required by law.

7. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. Ever.

  • Coaches and recruiters on the platform can see athlete profiles and performance scores — that is the purpose of the platform.
  • We use hosting and email providers to run the service; they process data only on our instructions.
  • We may share aggregated, anonymised statistics (for example, "average agility score by sport") with sports bodies — but only where you have given research consent, and never in a form that identifies you.
  • We may disclose data if required by a valid legal order.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our live database immediately. Anonymised aggregate statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify users inside the app.

10. Contact us

Questions, requests, or complaints about your data? Email athletixai0919@gmail.com and we will respond.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
