4. Important: what our AI scores mean (and do not mean)

AthletixAI provides estimates and guidance, not certified measurements. Our scores are generated by a rule-based biomechanics engine using body-keypoint data from your camera. They are useful for tracking relative progress over time, but they are not laboratory-grade measurements and must not be treated as an official assessment of your ability.

  • Scores can be affected by lighting, camera angle, clothing, and how much of your body is visible.
  • Predictions such as "talent potential", "medal probability" and "best-fit sport" are indicative estimates, not guarantees of any outcome, selection or opportunity.
  • The "injury risk" indicator is a general wellness signal only. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for a doctor or physiotherapist. Always consult a qualified professional for any pain, injury or medical concern.
